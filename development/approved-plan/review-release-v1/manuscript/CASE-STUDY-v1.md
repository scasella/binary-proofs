# Reusing production-binary contracts across three linked experimental callers

The case study establishes checked composition for three different final-linked experimental callers using the same supplier contracts for unchanged distribution-shipped `CRYPTO_memcmp` and `OPENSSL_cleanse`. The claims concern concrete loaded images and declared x86/E3/E5 models. They are accompanied by final-artifact binding, complete source reconstruction and retained unsuccessful construction history. The result supports bounded, repairable transfer of a supplier assurance package. It does not establish automatic caller integration, unrestricted hardware correspondence or construction-cost savings.

This manuscript evidence draft consolidates accepted sources. It introduces no new proof target or integration framework. Human review remains **PENDING**. The authoritative release identity, later checking-only consumption status and receipt digests are in `development/approved-plan/review-release-v1/release-index-v1.json`; historical packages retain their original identities. Priority and comparative novelty claims remain **UNVERIFIED** pending an actual related-work comparison.

## Mechanism and experimental boundary

The supplier is the unchanged Debian production libcrypto artifact with SHA-256 `76dd3d93e5ee48950a92a58d59b94de8143847f91a80d9682c938767b991577d`. Its two established public policies bind full-admissible-domain checked runs to fixed entries `0x10241840` and `0x102417d0`. Comparison establishes equality over initial logical bytes, with strict buffer access, finite actual return and ABI/frame preservation. Cleanup establishes zeros within the designated region and preservation outside its allowed effects. The separately accepted comparison trace property is architectural trace noninterference; none of the three callers receives a trace theorem merely by invoking that interface.

Each caller proves its actual instruction sequence and linkage. Immediate-relative CALL writes the real return slot; a six-byte RIP-relative PLT prefix reads a resolved eight-byte GOT target; execution reaches the frozen supplier entry. The caller derives the supplier precondition from that current state, applies the public policy, transports its consequences and composes the continuation. Public supplier contracts are unchanged. Caller construction is inspected for absence of supplier-body symbolic execution, while fresh checking reconstructs the complete supplier proofs from source. This distinction permits logical reuse without treating a stored theorem or assumed interface as proof.

The execution predicate requires a successor at each positive count and policy compliance for every original successor. It combines functional endpoints with intermediate full-width read/write/fetch constraints and the actual final RET transition. Frames protect incoming return, code/GOT and memory outside the declared write footprint. Qualified scalar/E5 rules lift these runs into the extended ABI state under RF=TF=0. The normal-entry correspondence closes the specific RF housekeeping mismatch within that domain; RF=1/debug-resume, exceptions and complete processor refinement remain outside it.

Loaded images and linkage are fixed after initialization. The dynamic loader's construction of the image, symbol binding/absence of interposition, ordinary stable memory and stated CPU/OS interpretation remain assumptions. Extraction and GNU/parser cross-checks are trusted mechanisms, not a verified ELF parser or loader. These assumptions do not assert the routine's functional result.

## What transferred and what did not

The same supplier contracts and their proof chain, parameterized checked-run composition, generic instruction/ABI machinery and E5 interpretation carry across the cases. Addresses, instruction-site arrays, call targets, permission/span instantiations, snapshots, branch states, saved-slot facts, frame cancellation and endpoint reasoning remain caller-specific work.

C2 also required integration adaptation: general permission/frame/projection/RET/word helpers and callback/normalization automation were created during its development. Candidate-layer preparation extracted ten address-free helper fragments and fixed parameter interfaces before selecting exact C3. Those costs are recorded as preparation adaptation; later freeze does not retroactively make C2 an unchanged-integration success. Accepted C1/C2 packages were not migrated. Additive regression packages reconstructed their unchanged targets against the candidate surface.

The candidate's 34 public theorem exports and algorithms were frozen before exact C3 selection. Its extension policy permits closed site facts, concrete stack/word data, caller predicates and ordinary local proof sequences. New universal integration lemmas or changed matching/callback/normalization behavior count as adaptation regardless of filename. Counts identify the audited surface; neither export/helper count nor bytes covered is a productivity or novelty measure.

## The failed held-out trial and accepted successor

The original C3 trial did not complete within its fixed 10,800-second construction budget. Outcome C, its frozen case, failed sources, timestamps, receipts and postmortem remain unchanged. A later separate successor was authorized with another 10,800-second budget and 1,800-second per-check limit. It began with knowledge of the exact case and diagnostics. This is a completion stage, not a fresh held-out replicate or revision of the original outcome.

The successor repaired caller-local proof failures: retaining a pointer/scratch premise at cleanup, using the actual u6 GOT fact and u7 state, transporting four saved slots and incoming return at `S`, deriving current code/GOT predicates, discharging alias-aware snapshot premises, executing the second handoff and correcting relation association in final-frame cancellation. It checked the actual whole chain rather than assuming a suffix entry or accepting conditional helper endpoints.

At fixed caller offset 73, `MOV EAX,2`, the generic convenience qualifier left an existing immediate-operand predicate opaque. The successor proved and applied a concrete site decode/class/fetch/event-policy fact through unchanged step primitives. The generic qualifier, callback, normalizer, exported statements, ISA/model and supplier contracts were not changed. This is inspection-classified permitted local work under predeclared clauses 3/4. It establishes qualification for that site, not automatic support for arbitrary MOV instances. Human review of that classification remains pending.

The accepted successor reconstructs all seven original whole/handoff/count/interface/E5 targets plus entry witnesses and their literal/expanded definitions. All four final paths reach actual RET. It is evidence that the candidate algorithms support this case after additional explicit local work. It is not a successful unchanged transfer within the original budget, an isolated no-exposure experiment or a claim of imminent/general caller completion.

## Checking and construction observations

The historical positive source-reconstruction runs have complete-wall measurements of 447.680 seconds for C1, 514.666 seconds for C2 and 591.303 seconds for C3 successor. Their supplier/caller phases are respectively 400.991/43.102, 399.053/109.832 and 454.672/129.618 wall seconds. These phases are nested within the full runs; they must not be added as extra independent costs. The complete root gates subsequently validate controls and acceptance. Later release-consumption checks have their own records.

Checking is not construction. Source-writing durations exclude reasoning; warm development intervals include failed searches, loads, waiting and coordination. The available ledgers preserve failures and report interval unions rather than double-counting concurrent inclusive measures. Historical supplier construction and complete marginal construction costs are unknown. Tokens/costs are null where unexposed. There is no matched interface-free baseline, stable productivity estimate, amortized saving or break-even calculation.

Historical checking uses native ARM HOL over a mathematical x86 model and emulated GNU x86 extraction on the same Apple Silicon host/Docker service. Release consumption should report precisely which relocation, filesystem, container, host/service and operator properties were exercised. Neither another agent nor a relocated local tree is independent human or external-organizational validation. Release consumption is checking-only; it cannot erase construction failures or extend their deadlines.
