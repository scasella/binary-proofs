# Publication scope

This edition publishes inspectable source and review evidence. It includes the current human review and manuscript packets, the release index, literal caller/contracts/freeze/class/extension sources, C1/C2/C3 caller source, checking scripts, complete compact consumer receipts, and selected original failure and postmortem records. An attempted proof source or saved receipt is not a certificate of whole-caller acceptance; consult the authoritative release index for its status.

The [selection manifest](publication-selection-v1.json) records the exact original paths, SHA-256 hashes, sizes, selection sources, and excluded candidates. Original files remain unchanged in the local accepted fork. The preserved supplier version rule uses the digest-checked BP-E1 archive and its indexed extra files; mutable historical top-level documents in that archive can differ from current documents. This edition preserves selected versions and does not claim to replace that materialization process.

**Full replay assets are NOT published. Replay asset acquisition is NOT presently complete.** The sealed checking and review archives, Docker runtime archive, supplier archive, compiled callers and library binaries, full Intel manuals and converted text, raw run source clones, and large preservation manifests remain local. Their identities are retained in the delivery and selection manifests. There is no working public download or complete public replay procedure in this edition.

In particular, these exact sealed assets are absent:

- `artifacts/checking-bundles/BP-E1-CHECKING-V2.tar.gz`
- `development/approved-plan/review-release-v1/delivery/checking-sources-v1.tar.gz`
- `development/approved-plan/review-release-v1/delivery/review-evidence-v1.tar.gz`
- `development/approved-plan/review-release-v1/delivery/docker-images-v1.tar`
- `development/approved-plan/review-release-v1/consumer/evidence-v1.tar.gz`

Historical checking/extraction commands are preserved as evidence of the sealed local delivery. They require omitted inputs and are not a runnable recipe for a fresh public clone. Recorded complete consumer gates establish the reported local checking result; downloading this edition does not reconstruct live theorem objects or repeat those gates.

Publishing the full sealed archives remains pending concrete redistribution authorization for the Intel 2016 manual and converted text, and completion of exact corresponding-source and notice obligations for the libc/runtime image. The original archives have not been modified to work around these obligations. License notices in this edition do not establish that all runtime redistribution obligations have been completed.

Human review is pending. The original capped held-out failure, separately accepted successor, and later checking-only consumption remain distinct. Comparative novelty and construction economics are unestablished.

Original receipts contain local home and temporary paths, hostnames, Docker/container identifiers, and socket paths. Those details are documentary evidence of the recorded environment; they do not confer independent-host, independent-Docker-service, or independent-operator validation. The original bytes have not been redacted or rewritten.
